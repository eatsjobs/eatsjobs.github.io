I'm Pasquale — `eatsjobs` in most places online. I build things for the web, and
this is where I'll write about them.

The short version: I'm a Senior Frontend Engineer at
[Scandit](https://www.scandit.com/), working on the Web SDK — barcode scanning
that runs in a browser, which is a more interesting constraint than it sounds.
Before that, eDreamsOdigeo here in Barcelona, luisaviaroma in Florence, and
Docomo Digital before that. Eleven-odd years of frontend, four of them at
Scandit.

I grew up in Tropea, on the Calabrian coast, studied in Rome and Florence, and
have been in Barcelona since 2019. Away from a screen I'm usually walking,
playing beach volleyball or football, swimming, or testing my neighbours'
patience with a guitar.

As for what lands here, I expect it to be some mix of:

- **The web platform itself**, and how far you can get using it directly before
  reaching for a framework. This site is a small argument for that position:
  hand-written HTML and CSS, a handful of ES modules, no bundler.
- **Web Components**, which I keep coming back to.
  [type-writer](https://github.com/eatsjobs/type-writer) is one of mine, and
  it's typing my name on the homepage right now.
- **Testing awkward things** — cameras, media devices, hardware you can't
  reasonably attach to CI.
  [media-mock](https://github.com/eatsjobs/media-mock) came out of exactly that
  problem.

No schedule and no newsletter. Just notes as I go.
